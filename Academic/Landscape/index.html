Design project

Landscape Planning of Jamuna River Basin

Objectives

  1. 01To analyse the existing planning scheme of the site and its impact on ecology.
  2. 02To identify the key issues of the site and propose strategies.
  3. 03To guide future planning strategies through landscape planning for conserving the environment and biodiversity.
