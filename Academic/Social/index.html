Survey research

A Study on Activity Pattern of University Students during Covid-19 Pandemic in Bangladesh

Objectives

  1. 01To identify the general activities of university students in Bangladesh during the non-lockdown period of enforced university closure.
  2. 02To identify the activities of university students in Bangladesh during the enforced COVID-19 lockdown.
  3. 03To find the relationships, if any, between activities and socio-economic condition.
