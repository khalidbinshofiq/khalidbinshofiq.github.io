Project 1 of 4Economic analysis

Long Run Analysis of Economic Structure of the Selected Regions in Bangladesh: A Shift Share Method

Districts
Meherpur, Chuadanga, Kushtia, Chapai Nawabganj, Rajshahi, Naogaon
Period
Base year 2003, terminal year 2013

Objectives

  1. 01To assess the regional economic growth of selected districts based on the shift-share components.
  2. 02To examine the effect of the selected economic sectors on regional growth based on the shift-share components.
  3. 03To find the possible reasons for positive and negative values of the shift-share components.

Work

  1. 01

    Shift-share analysis

  2. 02

    National growth vs regional growth rate

  3. 03

    Contribution of subsectors to regional growth

  4. 04

    Lagging and leading industries in the regions

  5. 05

    Inter-regional analysis

Project 2 of 4Formal regionalization

Formal Regionalization Based on Human Development Index: A District Level Analysis in Bangladesh

Study area
All 64 districts of Bangladesh

Objectives

  1. 01To calculate the HDI for all the districts of Bangladesh.
  2. 02To compare and highlight the levels of human development among the districts across the country.
  3. 03To explore the possible reasons for backward and developed regions based on the parameters of the HDI.

Work

  1. 01

    Methodology

  2. 02

    Formal region delineation

  3. 03

    Rank of divisions based on HDI value
