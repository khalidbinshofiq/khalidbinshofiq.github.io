Khalid-Bin-Shofiq

Urban & Regional Planner

I am an Urban Planner with professional experience in spatial planning, GIS, land-use analysis, transportation planning, accessibility, and urban development. My interests lie at the intersection of urban growth, GeoAI, transportation, land-use dynamics, and environmental sustainability, with particular emphasis on understanding how mobility, accessibility, development patterns, and environmental conditions interact in rapidly urbanizing regions. I am interested in pursuing advanced academic and research opportunities where geospatial analysis, data-driven methods, and interdisciplinary planning approaches can contribute to more sustainable, accessible, and resilient cities.
