📘 Title: Mapping Temporal Variation of TOD Potential Using ACT/PCT

Project Description

Dhaka is experiencing unplanned urbanization, population surge, and environmental degradation. A strategy to cope with these challenges that is practiced globally is “Transit Oriented Development (TOD)”. TOD helps to reduce automobile dependency, greenhouse gas emissions, and integrate public transport systems for better livability, inclusiveness, and sustainable development. Hence, TOD potential needs to be assessed.

Several contemporary studies have evaluated the TOD index, but temporal variation in this index has not been explored. This study will examine the changes in TOD potential over a decade using 5D indicators (Density, Diversity, Design, Destination Accessibility, Distance to Transit) and optimization techniques (Ant Colony Optimization and Particle Swarm Optimization).

Preparation of indicators across time intervals is in progress but not yet completed. After that, optimization will be implemented. Based on the comparative results, the study will support prioritizing investments in specific zones and recommending TOD-friendly policies to improve residents’ quality of life.
