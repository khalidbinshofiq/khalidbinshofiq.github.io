Ongoing Research · Transit-Oriented Development

Mapping Temporal Variation of TOD Potential Using ACT/PCT

This ongoing study investigates how Transit-Oriented Development potential in Dhaka may have changed over time by preparing spatial indicators based on the 5D framework — Density, Diversity, Design, Destination Accessibility and Distance to Transit. The current stage focuses on temporal indicator preparation and visualization. Ant Colony Optimization (ACO/ACT) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO/PCT) are planned for the subsequent analytical stage and have not yet been implemented.

Research Rationale

Why Temporal TOD Potential Matters

Conventional TOD assessment commonly produces a static index. This research is intended to examine how the spatial potential for TOD changes over time as population, built-up intensity, employment, land-use composition and accessibility conditions evolve.

Dhaka continues to experience rapid population growth, urban expansion, environmental pressure and increasing dependence on motorized travel. TOD offers a planning framework for concentrating growth around transit while improving accessibility and reducing automobile dependence. The central research question is therefore not only where TOD potential is high, but also how that potential changes over time.

Conceptual Framework

TOD Assessment Through the 5D Framework

The study is structured around the 5D logic of Density, Diversity, Design, Destination Accessibility and Distance to Transit. At this stage, the page presents the indicators that have already been prepared or selected without implying that the final TOD index has been calculated.

D

Density

Population density, built-up density and employment concentration.

D

Diversity

Land-use mix and the spatial balance of different urban functions.

D

Design

Built-environment and network characteristics supporting walkable access.

D

Destination Accessibility

Relative accessibility to major destinations and activity centres.

D

Distance to Transit

Spatial proximity to transit infrastructure and transit service opportunities.
