📝 Project: Mapping Temporal Variation of TOD Potential Using ACT/PCT

Dhaka is experiencing rapid and largely unplanned urbanization, accompanied by population growth and environmental degradation. A widely adopted strategy to address these challenges is Transit-Oriented Development (TOD), which aims to reduce automobile dependency, lower greenhouse gas emissions, and integrate public transportation systems to promote livability, inclusiveness, and sustainable urban development.

While several contemporary studies have assessed TOD potential using composite indices, the temporal variation of TOD potential has remained largely unexplored. This study investigates changes in TOD potential over a decade using the 5D framework—Density, Diversity, Design, Destination Accessibility, and Distance to Transit— combined with optimization techniques, including Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO).

The preparation of indicators across time intervals is currently underway. Following optimization and comparative analysis, the findings are expected to support prioritized investment strategies and TOD-friendly policy formulation aimed at improving residents’ quality of life.
