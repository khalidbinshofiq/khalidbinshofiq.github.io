📝 Project: Exploring the Influence of Potential Factors of Urban Change in Bangladesh.

Objectives

  1. To quantify the relative influence of socio-economic, environmental, and accessibility-related factors on built-up land expansion across Bangladesh.
  2. To evaluate and compare machine learning and statistical models for predicting regional patterns of urban growth.

Bangladesh is experiencing rapid urbanization driven by population dynamics, economic activity, topography, proximity to urban centers, transportation networks, and waterbodies. However, the relative contribution of these drivers to urban change remains insufficiently examined.

This study compares Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Artificial Neural Networks using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC–AUC metrics. Random Forest and XGBoost demonstrate superior performance, highlighting regional heterogeneity in urban growth processes.

Population, elevation, and distance to waterbodies are dominant drivers nationwide, while Barishal exhibits highway-oriented expansion constrained by river morphology—insights that support context-sensitive and sustainable urban planning.
