Origin–Destination Analysis

Internal Trip Structure & Regional Connectivity

The O–D survey used roadside interviews to capture trip origin, destination, purpose, vehicle occupancy and journey characteristics. The updated outputs include survey-location mapping, internal trip distribution, external district connections, the inter-union matrix and a spatial flow map.

5,289 union-wise trips recorded across the survey.
1,939 inter- and intra-union trips represented in the updated matrix.
148 largest individual inter-union flow: Bahirdia Mansa → Lakhpur.
524 trips received by Lakhpur, the strongest matrix destination.

Transport-planning insight: Fakirhat is not simply a local circulation system. The O–D evidence shows strong inter-union interaction together with external linkage, particularly toward Khulna and the wider south-western region.

Parking & Node Operations

Roadside Parking Is an Operational Constraint

Parking demand is concentrated around key activity and transport nodes. Short-duration stopping, passenger pick-up/drop-off and the absence of formal parking areas reduce effective road capacity around important intersections.

94% reported no specific parking area.
95% considered dedicated parking necessary.

Operations & Road Safety

Delay Is Localized Rather Than Corridor-Wide

Travel-time results distinguish slower, activity-affected sections from relatively free-flowing corridors. The safety assessment then connects those operating conditions with accident-prone locations, roadside activity and infrastructure deficiencies.

Planning Translation

From Survey Findings to Transport Interventions

The strength of the assignment is the translation of multiple survey streams into location-specific, multimodal and accessibility-oriented recommendations for the master-plan process.

Intersections

Target critical nodes

Improve circulation, turning movements and access management at Katakhali, Bishwaroad, Duckbangla and other major activity nodes.

Walking

Protect pedestrian movement

Provide crossings, footpaths, lighting and traffic-calming measures where pedestrian demand and vehicular conflict overlap.

Public Transport

Organize stopping and transfer

Formalize loading/unloading, public transport stands and passenger facilities at high-demand nodes.

Parking

Manage roadside stopping

Provide designated parking and short-duration pick-up/drop-off areas rather than allowing uncontrolled carriageway occupation.

Road Network

Strengthen core links

Improve strategic local-road connections and network redundancy between unions and the Upazila Headquarters.

Rural Access

Upgrade lower-order roads

Prioritize damaged, earthen and HBB village roads together with drainage, culverts and street lighting.

Rail

Improve multimodal potential

Strengthen Katakhali Railway Station passenger facilities and its integration with local road-based transport.

Road Safety

Apply hotspot-specific measures

Address speed, markings, visibility, highway-access conflicts, roadside markets and other conditions identified at accident-prone locations.
