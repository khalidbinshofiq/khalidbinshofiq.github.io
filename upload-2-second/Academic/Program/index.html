Trip Assignment: Identifying the Optimum Commuting Route

About the project

Trip assignment is the last phase of the four-step travel demand model. It allocates origin–destination (OD) trips to routes by mode and computes the link flows on a multimodal network, often posed as a demand–supply equilibrium problem.
