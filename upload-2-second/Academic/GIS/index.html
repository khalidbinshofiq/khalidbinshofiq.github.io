Project 1 of 2Network analysis

A GIS-based Network Analysis to Determine the Shortest Route and Service Area of Fire Stations to Hospitals for Emergency Response: A Case Study of Dhaka City Corporation

At a glance

23.5 minmean response time of fire stations

Objectives

  1. 01To identify the service area of existing fire stations.
  2. 02To find the shortest route for providing fire service from fire stations to hospitals.
  3. 03To determine the response time for assessing the service of fire stations.

Abstract

Fires at hospitals are a typical occurrence that frequently causes considerable health and property damage. Fire stations should be well equipped and prepared to give aid to hospitals in times of emergency. Response time is an important factor in determining the quality of a fire station's service, and it should be as short as possible. It depends on the condition of the emergency vehicle as well as any impediments or restrictions on the road network. Hospitals in Bangladesh are also prone to fire accidents, and in an emergency the current road network, congestion and unplanned land use make it difficult to provide an effective fire service. This study models the shortest route for a fire service through the road network using GIS and applies it to the tertiary hospitals of the Dhaka City Corporation area, so that fire stations can take the shortest possible route, reducing emergency response time and helping to save lives and property.
